handling feature requests

sometimes customers leave a review or contact support just to ask for something new - dark mode,
offline support, a specific integration, whatever. these aren't complaints and shouldn't be
treated like a problem to fix, but they also shouldn't be ignored.

log the request with enough detail that product can actually act on it later (what they want and
why, not just "wants dark mode"). thank the customer for the suggestion specifically, don't send
a generic "thanks for your feedback" that could apply to anything.

don't promise a timeline or even that the feature will happen at all - just that it's been passed
along. if the same request comes up a lot, mention that internally, since volume matters a lot
more for prioritization than any one request.

if a customer is asking for something that already exists but they haven't found it (this happens
a lot with settings that are buried in a menu), that's not actually a feature request - just
point them to where it already is.
