# Warranty Terms

## Coverage period

All physical products purchased through our store come with a **12-month limited warranty**
from the date of delivery, covering manufacturing defects and hardware failures that occur
under normal use.

## What is covered

- Component failure not caused by physical damage, water damage, or unauthorized modification.
- Battery capacity dropping below 70% of original within the warranty period, for products with
  a rechargeable battery.
- Manufacturing defects present at the time of delivery, even if not noticed immediately.

## What is not covered

- Cosmetic wear (scratches, scuffs) from normal use.
- Damage from drops, water exposure, or attempted self-repair.
- Products purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers.

## How to file a warranty claim

Customers should contact support with their order ID and a description of the issue. Support
will determine whether the issue falls under warranty coverage or should instead be routed as a
damaged-item or refund case (see the Damaged Item Resolution Guide for issues that occurred
during shipping rather than developing over time).

## Extended warranty

Not currently offered as a purchasable add-on. If a customer asks, let them know this may become
available in the future but is not currently an option.
