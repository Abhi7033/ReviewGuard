# Escalation Process

## When to escalate

- The customer has already contacted support 2+ times about the same unresolved issue.
- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a manager or escalate.
- The issue involves a safety concern (e.g. a product malfunction that could cause injury).
- Sentiment is strongly negative and the standard resolution options haven't satisfied them.

## How to escalate

1. Do not make the customer repeat their entire history - summarize the issue and prior attempts
   yourself when handing off.
2. Tag the ticket with the correct escalation reason so it routes to the right team (billing,
   technical, or general customer relations).
3. Set expectations with the customer: escalated tickets are reviewed within 1 business day, and
   they will hear back even if there's no resolution yet, so they aren't left wondering.

## What NOT to do

Do not repeatedly transfer a customer between agents without making progress - this is one of
the most common complaints we get and actively makes the situation worse. One clean handoff to
the right team beats several transfers.
