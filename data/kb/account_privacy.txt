Account Data and Privacy Notes

Customers occasionally ask about deleting their account or what data we keep. Account deletion
requests should be forwarded to the privacy team directly rather than handled in a normal support
conversation - support can confirm we received the request but shouldn't process it directly.

Order history is retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes even after an account is
deleted, though it's disassociated from any personally identifying info beyond what's legally
required to keep.

If a customer asks what data we have on them, that's a data access request and also goes to the
privacy team, not handled ad hoc by support.
