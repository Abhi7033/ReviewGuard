setting up your product - quick notes

ok so a few customers have said the included instructions are confusing, here's what support
should know to fill the gaps.

first thing - the quick start card in the box is intentionally minimal, it's not meant to cover
everything, full instructions are on the support site under "getting started" but customers don't
always find this on their own, so just link it directly instead of pointing them to "check the
website" which is too vague.

biggest confusion point by far is the initial pairing/setup step - customers often think the
product is broken when really it just needs a firmware update on first boot, which can take a
few minutes and doesn't show much on-screen feedback while it happens. tell people to just wait
it out, up to 5 minutes, before assuming something's wrong.

second biggest issue: people skip the app download step because the quick start card mentions it
last, when really it should happen first. if someone's stuck on setup, first question to ask is
whether they've downloaded the companion app yet, before troubleshooting anything else.

not really a formal FAQ doc, just things that keep coming up
