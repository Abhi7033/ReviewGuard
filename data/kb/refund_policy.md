# Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you're not, here's how refunds work.

## Eligibility

- Items must be requested for refund within **30 days** of delivery.
- The item does not need to be unused, but it should be in the condition you received it in
  unless the refund reason is "damaged" or "defective" (see our Damaged Item Guide).
- Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.

## How to request a refund

1. Contact support with your order ID and the reason for the refund.
2. Support will confirm eligibility and issue a refund authorization within 1 business day.
3. If a physical return is required, you'll receive a prepaid shipping label.
4. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us
   receiving the returned item (or immediately for "no return needed" cases like damaged goods).

## Partial refunds

If only part of an order is being refunded (e.g. one item in a multi-item order), the refund
amount will exclude any promotional discount that required the full order to qualify.

## Notes for support agents

Refunds tied to a billing error (double charge, failed payment still charged) should be
processed as a billing correction, not a standard refund - see the billing errors doc.
