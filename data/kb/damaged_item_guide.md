# Damaged Item Resolution Guide

Customers reporting an item arrived broken, cracked, or otherwise damaged should be handled
with priority - this is one of our top drivers of negative reviews.

## Step 1: Confirm the damage

Ask for a photo if the customer is willing to provide one, but **never block a resolution on
getting a photo**. If the customer describes clear physical damage (broken screen, cracked
housing, item stopped working immediately on arrival), proceed without requiring proof.

## Step 2: Offer a resolution

Default order of options to offer, in this order:
1. Free replacement, shipped immediately (before the damaged item is even returned).
2. Full refund, no return required, if the customer doesn't want a replacement.
3. Store credit + 10% bonus, only if the customer proactively asks for it instead of a refund.

## Step 3: Do not require the customer to return the damaged item

Unless fraud is suspected, damaged items do not need to be shipped back. Asking a customer to
pay return shipping on something that arrived broken is a common complaint - avoid it.

## Timeline

Replacements should ship within 1 business day of the resolution being agreed. Refunds for
damaged items are processed immediately, not on the standard 5-7 day refund timeline.
