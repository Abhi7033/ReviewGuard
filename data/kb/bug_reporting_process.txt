App Crashes and Bug Reports

When a customer reports the app crashing, freezing, or a specific feature not working (like
uploads failing), the goal is to get enough detail for engineering without turning the support
conversation into a debugging session the customer didn't sign up for.

Ask for: device type and OS version, app version if they know it, and what they were doing right
before the crash. Don't ask for anything beyond that unless engineering specifically requests it
for a known issue.

If it matches a known issue already being tracked, tell the customer that directly - "we're aware
of this and a fix is in progress" - rather than opening a fresh investigation. If it's a new
report, file it and let the customer know it's been logged, but don't promise a fix timeline
since we don't control the engineering backlog from support.

For crashes tied to a specific action (uploading a file, opening a certain screen), a temporary
workaround is worth offering if one exists, even if it's not a real fix - customers generally
appreciate being unblocked now over waiting for a proper fix later.
