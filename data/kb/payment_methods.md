# Accepted Payment Methods

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover),
PayPal, and Apple Pay / Google Pay on supported devices.

## Saved payment methods

Customers can save a payment method to their account for faster checkout. Saved cards are
tokenized and we never store raw card numbers.

## Currency

All prices are displayed and charged in USD. International customers should expect their bank
to apply its own currency conversion and any associated fees - we don't control this.

## Gift cards

Gift cards can be applied at checkout and combined with one other payment method for the
remaining balance. Gift card balances do not expire.
