# Order Tracking FAQ

**Q: How do I check where my order is?**
A: Every order gets a tracking link emailed once it ships. If a customer says they never got it,
resend it rather than asking them to dig through their inbox or spam folder.

**Q: The tracking page hasn't updated in several days. Is something wrong?**
A: Not necessarily - some carriers batch scan updates and can show no movement for 2-3 days
even when the package is fine. Only treat this as a real problem past 5 days of no movement.

**Q: Can I change the delivery address after the order has shipped?**
A: No, once a package has left our warehouse we can't redirect it. If the customer needs to
change an address, this has to happen before the order ships - check order status first.

**Q: The tracking says "delivered" but the customer says they never received it.**
A: This is treated as a lost package, not a delivery dispute with the customer. Offer a reship
or refund without requiring the customer to prove anything further - "delivered" tracking
statuses are wrong often enough that we don't make this the customer's problem to solve.
