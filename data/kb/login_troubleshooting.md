# Login & Account Access Troubleshooting

Use this when a customer says they can't log in, especially right after an app or site update.

## Quick checks, in order

1. Confirm they're using the correct email (not a secondary email they may have used to place
   an order as a guest).
2. Ask them to try a password reset, even if they're confident their password is correct - a
   surprising number of "can't log in" cases are actually expired sessions being mistaken for
   login failures.
3. If they say login broke immediately after updating the app, this is very likely a known issue
   with cached session tokens. Ask them to fully log out (not just close the app) and log back in.
4. Check if their account was flagged for suspicious activity - this can silently block login
   without a clear error message on the customer's end, which is confusing and frustrating.

## When to escalate

If none of the above resolves it within one exchange, escalate to technical support rather than
continuing to troubleshoot back and forth - repeated failed login is high-frustration and should
not be dragged out.
