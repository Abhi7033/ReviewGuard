Billing Errors - Internal Notes

Double charges and failed-payment-but-still-charged cases come up often enough that they get
their own process instead of going through the normal refund flow.

If a customer says they were charged twice for the same order, first check whether it's actually
two separate orders (this happens more than you'd think) before assuming it's an error. If it is
a genuine duplicate charge, the extra charge should be reversed within 2 business days, not the
usual 5-7 day refund window - this is a billing correction, not a refund, and customers should be
told that explicitly so they don't think we're being slow.

Same urgency applies when a customer's bank shows a charge went through but our system shows the
payment failed and the order was never placed. This is usually a temporary authorization hold
that will drop off on the bank's side within a few days, but if the customer is anxious about it,
escalate to billing so they can confirm directly rather than telling the customer to "just wait
and see."

Do not tell customers to dispute the charge with their bank as a first step - that triggers a
chargeback process on our end that's more painful for everyone than just fixing it directly.
