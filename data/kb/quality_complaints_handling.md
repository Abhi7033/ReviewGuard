# Handling Build Quality Complaints

This covers complaints like "I expected better quality for this price" or "feels cheap" -
distinct from damaged items, since nothing is actually broken, the customer is just unhappy with
the product itself.

## Approach

- Don't be defensive about the product. Acknowledge the feedback as valid even if you personally
  disagree - the customer's experience is the customer's experience.
- Ask what specifically fell short (material, weight, finish, a specific component) if they
  haven't said - this feedback is genuinely useful to product and shouldn't be lost.
- This is not automatically a refund situation. Only offer a refund if the customer asks, or if
  the complaint is severe enough that it's clearly a quality control miss rather than a
  subjective preference.

## What not to do

Don't argue that the product "is actually good quality" or cite specs at the customer - this
reads as dismissive. The goal is to log the feedback and, if the customer wants a resolution,
offer one - not to convince them they're wrong.
