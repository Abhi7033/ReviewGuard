# Shipping Delay FAQ

**Q: My order is taking longer than expected. What's the standard delivery window?**
A: Standard shipping is 5-7 business days. Expedited shipping is 2-3 business days. Delays
beyond these windows can happen during peak season (holidays) or due to carrier issues outside
our control.

**Q: It's been more than 7 business days and I haven't received my order. What do I do?**
A: Apologize first, then look up the order status. If tracking shows the package is genuinely
lost or hasn't moved in 5+ days, offer either a full reship at no cost or a full refund -
customer's choice. Do not make them wait longer to "see if it turns up."

**Q: Can I get compensation for a late delivery, even if the item eventually arrived?**
A: Yes, if the delay exceeded 7 business days beyond the promised window. Offer a partial refund
of shipping cost, or a discount code for their next order (10%), depending on what the customer
prefers.

**Q: What if a customer is upset about a delay but the item hasn't technically missed its window
yet?**
A: Acknowledge the frustration, give an honest update on tracking status, and don't promise an
exact arrival date we can't guarantee. Under-promise.
